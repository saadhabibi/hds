Frequently asked

Questions about the funnel, GBP, SEO and websites

Which is the best digital marketing agency?
The best agency is the one that connects your services into a single system instead of selling them one by one. That's what we focus on — GBP, website, SEO and ads working together as a funnel, not isolated tasks.
What digital marketing services do you offer?
Google Business Profile setup and optimization, local SEO, website development, social media management, and Meta Ads — each mapped to a stage of the customer funnel: Discover, Trust, or Convert.
What is included in Google Business Profile setup and optimization?
Profile creation and verification, business info and category setup, photos, call/WhatsApp buttons, SEO optimization for ranking, and regular Google Business posts to keep the profile active.
How much does digital marketing cost?
Monthly plans start at ₹5,000. The right plan depends on how competitive your market is and how many funnel stages need active work — we recommend one after a free consultation.
How does a website help businesses get more inquiries?
A website is the "Trust" stage of the funnel — it's where someone who already found you decides whether to actually contact you. A fast, clear site with a visible call/WhatsApp button converts far more visitors than a slow or confusing one.
How long does GBP and website setup take?
Most setups are live within 3–7 days, depending on how much content and how many pages are involved.
Can you manage my Google Business Profile monthly?
Yes — all three monthly plans include ongoing GBP posts, optimization, and reporting, so the profile stays active instead of going stale after setup.
Do you work with all types of businesses?
We mostly work with shops, clinics, coaching centers, schools, and local service businesses — anywhere a customer searches on Google before making a decision.
